**Kloud Kart 12-month data roadmap (January to December)**

**Q1 (Jan to Mar)**: Stop the bleeding. Data Engineer starts
1\.	Before the 90 days run out, Nana can hand the Data Protection Commission a written list of every place the deleted customer’s data was held and checks it against the customer-list files Marketing knows about.

2\.	By 31 January, Nana and Finance’s lead hold the same signed one-page definition of revenue, and Nana holds a note saying whether Kloud Kart is registered with the Commission, with her as owner. She checks by reading both pages.

3\.	By 12 March, Nana opens her Monday report herself with no export, and customer service logs no slow-site complaint between 07:00 and 10:00 on four Mondays running.

**Q2 (Apr to Jun)**: One number. Analytics Engineer starts
1\.	By 31 May, Nana and Finance’s lead quote the same weekly revenue figure from the same report on four Mondays running. She checks by putting the two figures side by side.

2\.	By 30 June, Nana watches an invented customer deleted in the shop and sees within seven days that they appear in no report. Marketing’s lead also shows her a log of every customer-list export since April that matches the files on hand.

**Q3 (Jul to Sep)**: Trust
1\.	By 31 August, Nana gets a message by 07:00 each Monday saying “checked” or “do not use”, and sees “do not use” arrive after a failure staged on purpose.

2\.	By 30 September, Nana filters the Monday report by country and sees Nigeria beside Ghana, with delivery country recorded on every order from 1 July. The report also shows the monthly count of oversold orders, with a reduction target she sets.

**Q4 (Oct to Dec)**: \*Decide what’s next\*
1\.	By 30 November, Nana’s Monday report arrives on schedule in a week when the original developer is away.

2\.	By 15 December, Nana sees one forecasting trial on one product category set against a pass mark she agreed beforehand, ending in stop or go. It runs only if the Q2 and Q3 outcomes are met.

3\.	By 31 December, Nana gives the board one page with three numbers: database size against the 200GB review point, the time each morning’s figures are ready, and Nigeria’s order volume.


Dates assume each hire starts at the beginning of their quarter. If one is late, that quarter’s dates move by the same delay
